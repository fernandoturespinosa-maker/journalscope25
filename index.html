About the data and ranking. Journal metadata comes from Crossref. ChemRxiv is retrieved from its public API where accessible, with a Crossref DOI-prefix fallback; bioRxiv metadata and abstracts come from its public API. The bioRxiv search is focused on five relevant subject categories and retrieves up to 120 recent records per category. Repository searches follow the selected date window; “Any date retrieved” uses a bounded 90-day preprint window. Preprints have not necessarily been peer reviewed and are labelled separately. Relevance is a transparent keyword-based screening aid, not an assessment of scientific quality; check the paper itself before drawing conclusions. The briefing is generated locally and does not use an AI service. DOI links open the repository or article landing page; full-text access depends on the source. Retrieval is sequential and cached in this browser.
