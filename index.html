About the data. Article details come from Crossref metadata. Abstracts appear only when deposited with Crossref. DOI links open the article landing page; full-text access depends on the publisher. To respect Crossref's public API limits, the app checks journals one at a time and automatically waits and retries when the service is busy. A full 25-journal refresh may take around 30–45 seconds. A successful journal query with no matching records is different from a connection failure.
