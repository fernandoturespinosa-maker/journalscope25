About the data and ranking. Article details come from Crossref metadata. Abstracts and subject keywords appear only when deposited with Crossref. Relevance is a transparent keyword-based screening aid, not an assessment of scientific quality; check the paper itself before drawing conclusions. The briefing is generated locally from retrieved records and does not use an AI service. DOI links open the article landing page; full-text access depends on the publisher. To respect Crossref's public API limits, the app checks journals one at a time and automatically waits and retries when the service is busy. A full 25-journal refresh may take around 30–45 seconds under normal conditions.
